See the sources page for the full list and how each is collected.

Topic tags and picks come from an automated classifier that reads each title and abstract; nothing else on the page is machine-written. Abstracts are the authors' own, reproduced from publisher metadata (via Crossref, OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar) for discovery purposes, as on those services. Follow the link for the paper itself.

Zotero. Every entry carries citation metadata. With the Zotero Connector installed, click its toolbar icon on any issue page to save one or many papers at once, or subscribe to the paper feed as a Zotero feed.

AI assistants. Paste the address of the guide for AI assistants into your assistant with the request "Run the {{ site.title }} custom digest workflow" to get a personalized reading list from the current issue.
